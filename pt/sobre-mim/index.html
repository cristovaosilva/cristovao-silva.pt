O que faço

Vamos falar

Research, always

Good products are not built on instinct alone. I research your market, competitors, and users so every recommendation is backed by evidence, not guesswork. The result is clearer direction, more confident decisions, and less risk before development begins.

Code-ready prototypes

I build usable React prototypes from the start. That means less ambiguity, smoother collaboration with developers, and fewer rounds wasted trying to imagine how a flow should behave from a static file. It also leads to faster handoff and a more efficient development process.

Scalable design systems

I build design systems that are ready to grow with your product. They accelerate prototyping, keep expansion consistent, and reduce rework as new screens, features, and flows are added — while creating a stronger foundation for AI-supported workflows over time.

Clarity before execution

I help teams get clear on what needs to be built before time is lost in design or development. That means sharper priorities, fewer unnecessary iterations, and more confidence in the decisions that move the product forward.
